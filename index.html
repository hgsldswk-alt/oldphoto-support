Help

Scan an album page. Lay it flat in even light, fit the page in the viewfinder and hold still. Photo frames are detected automatically. You can also tap the shutter or import an image of the page.

Adjust a frame or reduce glare. Open a photo from the tray and tap Edit frame. For glare, change the lighting or follow the retake prompts if offered.

Keep a copy. Enable Auto save to Photos, or use Export at full resolution in the photo menu. Images already saved to Apple Photos stay there if you delete the app. In-app files and notes are stored on this iPhone.

Trial and purchases. Pro tools are available for 30 days from first launch. A one-time purchase unlocks them afterward. Scanning, browsing and full-resolution export remain available. For an earlier purchase, open Albums, then Settings, then Restore Purchases.

Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026. We do not collect personal information through the app. There is no account, developer-operated server, advertising or analytics.

The camera is used to scan printed photos and their backs. Scans, text recognition, dates, and notes stay on your iPhone. The app requests Add Only permission to save copies to Photos. You decide which images to import using the system picker. Copies in Photos follow your Apple Photos and iCloud settings.

A place search you initiate uses Apple Maps. Apple handles App Store payments and restoration. If you choose to share, the selected photos, cards or codes are sent to the app or people you choose. You can delete local scans in the app. An email sent to support is used to answer your request.
